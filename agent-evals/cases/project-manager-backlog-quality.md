# Case: project-manager-backlog-quality

## Goal

Measure whether the `project_manager` is producing a backlog that is executable, not just plausible.

## What To Evaluate

- presence of backlog structure beyond story list
- product capabilities are explicit
- epics are explicit
- release slicing is explicit
- user stories cover product foundation
- no truncation or obvious duplication
- persona diversity is acceptable

## Pass Signals

- `Visao Geral`, `Capacidades do Produto`, `Epicos Recomendados`, `Fatias de Release` and `Historias de Usuario` all exist
- story count is within expected range
- backlog includes `MVP` and later slices
- foundation coverage is strong enough to support delivery planning
- no truncated stories

## Failure Signals

- only a list of stories with no planning structure
- too many advanced edge cases before product foundation
- low foundation coverage
- duplicated or truncated stories
- release planning absent
